    About this guide

    Every step is summarised from the guidelines of ANZCOR, the Australian and New Zealand Committee on Resuscitation, as published on anzcor.org and checked in November 2026. Each topic shows the guideline it comes from and when that guideline was approved. The illustrations are simplified diagrams, not exact technique.

    The 3D body views use anatomy from BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science, licensed under CC Attribution 4.0 International, simplified from the Human Atlas viewer. The explanations beside them are general anatomy, not part of the ANZCOR guidelines.

    Each topic shows its review status: whether a qualified first aid trainer or clinician has reviewed it, and when. Until then, check anything you're unsure of with a first aid course or the source guideline.

    This is a quick reference. It doesn't replace a hands-on first aid and CPR course, and it isn't medical advice for a particular person. In an emergency, call 000 and follow the call-taker's instructions.

    Symbols and type

    Menu symbols are original drawings for this guide: a 2px geometric stroke and a light duotone fill, in the manner of Health Icons, which are published under CC0. These marks are not copied from that set. Type is Inter and Atkinson Hyperlegible, self-hosted under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
