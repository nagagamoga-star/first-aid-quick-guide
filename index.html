About this guide

Every step is summarised from the guidelines of ANZCOR, the Australian and New Zealand Committee on Resuscitation, as published on anzcor.org and checked in November 2026. Each topic shows the guideline it comes from and when that guideline was approved. The illustrations are simplified diagrams, not exact technique.

This is a quick reference. It doesn't replace a hands-on first aid and CPR course, and it isn't medical advice for a particular person. In an emergency, call 000 and follow the call-taker's instructions.
