**W (when to act):** **Start CPR** if the person isn’t responding and isn’t breathing normally. Occasional gasps are not normal breathing.

**CPR depth table:** Adult: heel of one hand on the centre of the chest (lower half of the breastbone), other hand on top; at least 5 cm. Child 1 year and over: same spot, one or two hands; about 5 cm. Baby under 1: same spot, two fingers; about 4 cm. All: about one-third of the chest depth.
